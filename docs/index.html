Features / Fonctionnalités

  • Player status: health, armor, hunger, thirst, stress
  • Money display: cash and bank
  • Job information with label
  • Voice indicators: range, talking, radio (pma-voice)
  • Vehicle info: speedometer, seatbelt, indicators, hazards, limiter, cruise control
  • Customizable HUD layout: move, resize, show/hide each element, streamer mode
  • Settings saved locally (KVP)
  • Seatbelt: ejection on violent crash, reduced injuries with belt
  • Cinema mode (F10)
  • Fuel warning and estimated range
  • Real or fake odometer
  • Multilingual: French and English

Installation

  1. Copy the nocturne_hud folder into your resources/ directory.
  2. Add ensure nocturne_hud to your server.cfg.
  3. Disable your old HUD (qb-hud, ps-hud, esx_hud…) to avoid duplicates.
  4. Configure config.lua.
